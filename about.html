About Tess

Certified positive reinforcement trainer, VSA-CDT

My name is Tess Cooper, and I am the owner of TC Pets.

TC Pets began in 2019 when I started my own dog walking company after spending just over a year walking dogs for another business in Manchester. While I loved spending time with all the wonderful dogs I met, the travel costs were almost as much as I was earning. So, with a heavy heart—but excited by the promise of meeting new furry friends—I decided to start my own business closer to home.

Today, I provide dog walking, training, and behaviour services across South Manchester and Cheshire East. I also offer online consultations and training sessions for clients who live outside the area but would still like to work with me.

I have completed several courses in Canine Behaviour and Canine First Aid before earning my Level 4 Diploma in Canine Behaviour and Dog Training, which I completed in early summer 2021. In 2024, I also completed the Victoria Stilwell Academy In-Person Dog Trainer Course. I continue to develop my knowledge and skills through ongoing education and professional development whenever possible.

I have a particular passion for rescued animals and helping both dogs and their families build happy, lasting relationships. My own dog, Casper, was rescued from Romania and adopted through Manchester Dogs' Home. He now enjoys a wonderfully spoiled life, joining TC Pets group walks, playing and sniffing with his many doggy friends, and receiving plenty of love and cuddles at home.

Through TC Pets Training, my goal is to help other dogs and their families enjoy the same trusting, rewarding relationship that Casper and I share.
