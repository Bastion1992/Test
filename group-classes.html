Group training classes

Learn together, grow together

Training classes are a group training setting, with a lesson plan we follow to progress as a group. For picking up the basics at Puppy Class, or starting out with a new activity like Treibball, group classes are the way to go, as everyone will be on the same page. You might even make some friends to practice with outside of classes too.

Book a class Get in touch

Our classes cover

Foundations for young puppies. Settling into the world, basic manners, and building confidence around people, dogs, and everyday situations.

For dogs past the puppy stage who need a refresh on the basics. Loose lead walking, recall, and calm behaviour around distractions.

A fun, low pressure session focused on teaching new tricks. Great for building confidence and strengthening the bond between dog and owner.

Dogs push large exercise balls into a goal, similar to herding sheep. Great mental and physical exercise for high energy or herding breeds.

How do I know which type of training to choose?

Group classes suit picking up basics or starting a new activity together. One to one sessions are tailored to your dog, home and lifestyle if you need a more individual pace.
