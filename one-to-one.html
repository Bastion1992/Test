One to one training

Personalised sessions, at your dog's own pace

One to one sessions are tailored entirely to your dog, your home, and your lifestyle. Rather than following a group lesson plan, we build a training plan around what you and your dog actually need, whether that is settling in a new puppy, working through specific behaviours, or building confidence at your own pace.

Every one to one journey starts with a Pre-Consultation form, so we can understand your dog and what you would like to work on before the first session. From there, sessions are booked in and built around your dog's progress, rather than a fixed group timetable.

Sessions start from £75. Package options are also available, see the Membership page for full details on Basic, Standard, and Premier packages.

Not sure if one to one is right for you?

Group classes suit picking up basics or starting a new activity together. One to one sessions are tailored to your dog, home and lifestyle if you need a more individual pace.
